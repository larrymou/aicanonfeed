Include items that report **AI industry business events**: financing, M&A, partnerships, market/revenue data, or non-model product launches, with a link to a company announcement, deal announcement, or reputable media report. If the item is a model/capability release **with an official owner announcement**, use R1 instead; if it is open-source tooling, use R5. Media-only coverage of a model launch (no official owner link) stays under R3.
