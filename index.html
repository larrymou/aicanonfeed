Active Rules

  • R1 Model Releases
    A post is included in model-releases when it reports a new model, major version update, or significant new capability released by an AI company, research lab, or official product owner, and links to the official or original announcement.
    Disagree? Propose a rule change
  • R2 Research
    A post is included in research when it reports a peer-reviewed or preprint paper from a recognized venue or research lab, an official technical report, or verifiable benchmark results, and links to the original source.
    Disagree? Propose a rule change
  • R3 Industry
    A post is included in industry when it reports financing, M&A, partnerships, product launches, or market data in the AI industry, and links to a company announcement, deal announcement, or reputable media report.
    Disagree? Propose a rule change
  • R4 Policy
    A post is included in policy when it reports AI policy, regulatory action, or compliance events issued by official bodies, or links to reputable coverage of such an event.
    Disagree? Propose a rule change
  • R5 Tools & OSS
    A post is included in tools-oss when it reports the initial release or major update of an open-source project or developer tool directly in the AI ecosystem, and links to the repository, release page, or official page.
    Disagree? Propose a rule change
