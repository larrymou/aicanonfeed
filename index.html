For this rule, "major version" means a version the owner labels as major on the announcement page, or a version number of the form N.0 (N >= 1). For this rule, "new model capability" means a change to what the model can do (new modality, tool use, or context-length class), not a UI, pricing, hiring, or partnership update. 3. Evidence link. The item links to either (a) an official announcement page on the owner's site (blog, model card, or release page), or (b) a full news article on a domain that is not the owner's domain, with a named author and a publication date. About · CANON

What this is — The first live CANON instance (github.com/larrymou/canon): constituents legislate inclusion rules on this repo, an AI editor applies only ratified rules, and GitHub publishes one shared 5-day window for everyone. Not a recommender.

Why this exists — Three failures we design against:

  • AI recommendation builds filter bubbles. Engagement-optimized feeds learn your clicks and quietly narrow what you see. Personalization is the product; the bubble is the side effect.
  • Human curation is opaque and subjective. Editorial picks often rest on unwritten taste. Readers cannot inspect the rule that included a story — only the outcome.
  • CANON is the third path. Rules are legislated in public, an AI editor applies only what was ratified, and every call lands in decisions/. Not “trust the model” and not “trust the desk” — trust the process.

Designed forward — Rules and quotas evolve through CANON; we do not rewrite history to match.

CANON (paradigm: github.com/larrymou/canon) in three steps here — community legislates on Issues, AI judges only by enacted rules, and GitHub (code + history) is the neutral executor.

01
Legislate
Open a rule proposal via GitHub Issues. Bound by meta-rules M1–M7 (M1 complete form · M2 objective wording · M5 no edits to voting mechanics — plain-language table in CONTRIBUTING).
02
Adjudicate
AI pre-reviews proposals and applies only ratified rules in rules/ — it never invents inclusion criteria.
03
Execute
Votes, PRs, and decisions/ are public. No ranking, no pins — every inclusion can be audited.

How to join — Propose a rule, vote 👍 / 👎 on issues labeled voting, or audit calls under decisions/. Full guide: CONTRIBUTING.md. Rules take ~8–14 days.

  • Strict reverse-chronological order. Tabs are filters, not rankings.
  • Research is capped on ingest and again on the page so industry/policy/tools stay visible.
  • Uncovered items are rejected by design.
  • Meta-rule M7 forbids personalization: no per-user feeds, no engagement ranking.
  • Rules change over time; past decisions keep the rule version in effect when they were made.
  • CANON experiment: voting parameters (quorum, F1, proposal quota, …) are code-locked for now — more of them are meant to move to community votes as the loop matures.
